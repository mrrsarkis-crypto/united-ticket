Burbank ticket questions, answered

Where do Burbank traffic tickets go to court?

Most Burbank citations are heard at the Burbank Courthouse (300 E. Olive Ave.). Your ticket lists the exact courthouse and due date — scan it with our free tool and we'll confirm where your case will be heard.

Can I fight a Burbank ticket without going to court?

In most cases, yes. We prepare a trial by written declaration (form TR-205), which lets you contest the ticket entirely by mail. No courtroom appearance is required for the written trial itself.

How much does it cost to fight a ticket in Burbank?

Our fee is a flat $199, no matter the violation. That covers document preparation, attorney review before filing, and case tracking through the decision.

Will a Burbank ticket raise my insurance?

A moving violation conviction in California typically adds a point to your DMV record, and insurers often raise premiums for several years after a point goes on your record. If the ticket is dismissed, no point is added.
