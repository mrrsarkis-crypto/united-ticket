Pasadena ticket questions, answered

Where are Pasadena traffic tickets heard?

Pasadena citations are generally heard at the Pasadena Courthouse, 300 E. Walnut St. Your ticket shows the assigned courthouse — scan it free and we'll verify the court and your deadline.

Can I do traffic school instead of fighting my Pasadena ticket?

Sometimes. If you're eligible for traffic violator school, it can keep the point off your record — but you still pay the full fine plus school fees, and you can only use it once every 18 months. Fighting the ticket costs a flat $199 and, if dismissed, there's no fine at all. Our free scan helps you compare.

How long do I have to respond to a Pasadena ticket?

Your ticket lists an appearance date, usually several weeks out. Don't wait until the last day — a trial by written declaration takes preparation time, and missing the date can add a failure-to-appear charge.

Do red light camera tickets in Pasadena count as points?

Yes — a red light camera conviction in California is a moving violation that adds a point to your DMV record, just like being pulled over. Camera tickets are very defendable on procedural grounds, which is why many drivers challenge them.
