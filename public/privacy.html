    Privacy Policy

    We treat your citation photos and personal information as sensitive data. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we protect it, and how you can have it deleted.

    The short version

    We collect only what we need to prepare, review, and track your citation documents. We never sell your personal information. Citation photos, driver's-license information, and dates of birth are treated as sensitive and are encrypted in transit and protected at rest. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

    What we collect

    • Ticket photo(s) you upload for scanning and document preparation.
    • Driver's-license information (number) and date of birth, when provided, to prepare accurate paperwork.
    • Contact details — name, email, phone, and optional mailing address.
    • Case details — citation number, court, violation code, dates, amounts, and notes you provide.
    • Supporting documents you choose to upload.
    • Basic technical data such as device/browser type, needed to operate and secure the site.

    How we use it

    • To scan and review your citation and identify potential issues.
    • To prepare and review the documents you request.
    • To email you your prepared documents and receipts, and case status.
    • To comply with legal obligations and operate our business securely.

    How we store and protect it

    Ticket photos, driver's-license information, dates of birth, and supporting documents are stored in access-controlled storage. Access is restricted to staff who need it to prepare or review your case. Payments are handled by a PCI-compliant processor (Stripe); we do not store your full card details.

    Cookies, advertising, and Google AdSense

    We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, understand how visitors use it, and to display third-party advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use advertising cookies (including, where applicable, the Google Advertising Cookie and the DoubleClick Cookie) to serve and personalize ads based on your visits to this site and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad, and to measure advertising performance.

    • Essential cookies are required for the site to function (for example, to keep your session and remember your scroll position in the ticket-upload flow).
    • Analytics cookies help us understand aggregate usage so we can improve the site.
    • Advertising cookies are used by Google AdSense to deliver and measure ads. They are only loaded on pages that display advertising (not on the ticket-upload, payment, or case-tracking flow).

    You can learn more about how Google collects and uses data, and how to control it, at Google's "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" and the Google Ads/AdSense privacy options.

    Opting out of personalized advertising: you can opt out of personalized advertising from Google through Google's Ads Settings, and through the NAI / industry opt-out pages linked there. Where required by law or Google policy, we obtain your consent before loading advertising cookies, and we honor your choice via a consent banner on the site.

    Our ads never run in your private flow

    We deliberately do not place advertising inside or directly beside the ticket-upload, payment, or case-tracking steps. Advertising appears only on informational pages.

    Rights under the CCPA/CPRA (California residents)

    If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) give you rights to:

    • Know what personal information we collect, use, and share.
    • Access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
    • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
    • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising in a way that would trigger a sale under the CCPA.
    • Correct inaccurate personal information we hold about you.
    • Non-discrimination — we will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

    We do not sell the personal information of any resident.

    How long we keep it

    We retain case records for as long as needed to provide the service and to satisfy legal/record-keeping obligations, and for a reasonable period afterward. You may request deletion earlier (see below). We delete personal data when it is no longer needed for these purposes.

    Your rights

    You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To do so, contact us with your name and the email or tracking code used for your case. We will act on verified requests within a reasonable time. If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, you may also contact your state's data-protection authority (in California, the California Privacy Protection Agency).

    Updates

    We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.

    Contact
