West Covina drivers get tickets in the same handful of spots

Between San Gabriel Valley commute traffic on the I-10 (San Bernardino Freeway), the shopping corridors along Azusa Avenue and Vincent Avenue, and enforcement near the freeway interchanges, West Covina writes a steady stream of speeding, red light, and cell phone citations. Here's what surprises West Covina drivers: the fine printed on the ticket is only the beginning. Paying the ticket can quietly cost far more than the fine once years of higher insurance are factored in. Contesting it is often the cheaper move.

West Covina ticket questions, answered

Where do West Covina traffic tickets go to court?

Most West Covina citations are heard at the West Covina Courthouse (1427 W. Covina Pkwy.). Your ticket lists the exact courthouse and due date — scan it with our free tool and we'll confirm where your case will be heard.

Can I fight a West Covina ticket without going to court?

In most cases, yes. We prepare a trial by written declaration (form TR-205), which lets you contest the ticket entirely by mail. No courtroom appearance is required for the written trial itself.
