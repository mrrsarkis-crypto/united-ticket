Fight the Ticket or Take Traffic School?

A quick, honest comparison so you can decide what's right for your situation. Every case is different — your scan is never a guarantee of any outcome.

Fight the Ticket

  • No points on your record if the citation doesn't hold — no impacts on insurance rates.
  • No school fees or classes — the fine may be reduced or dismissed if procedural issues are found.
  • Protects CDL and commercial drivers — many don't qualify for traffic school at all.
  • Challenges the violation itself instead of just masking it with a class.
  • Takes more time — court dates, paperwork, and waiting for the judge's decision.
  • No guarantee — you can still lose, pay the full fine, and add points.
  • Court-appearance costs add up if you're handling it yourself with attorney appearances.
  • Risk if you ignore it — missing court can mean a bench warrant or a suspended license.

Best when there are visible issues on the citation — missing court info, wrong vehicle info, officer errors — or when your record can't absorb more points.

Take Traffic School

  • Keep the points off your record — the DMV keeps your driving record clean once the school is completed.
  • Usually cheaper and faster — one class, a certificate, and it's done.
  • Can be done online — most courts accept state-approved online courses.
  • Less risk — no need to appear in court or argue the citation.
  • You still pay the fine — traffic school doesn't dismiss the ticket.
  • Available only once per 18 months for most violations, and it's at the court's discretion.
  • Not available for every violation — reckless driving, most CDL offenses, and DUI-related charges don't qualify.
  • Admission of responsibility — completing school is an acknowledgement of the violation.

Best when you simply want the points hidden and the matter closed — or when the citation carries no fight-worthy defect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our service, privacy, and what to expect.

Is this a law firm or legal advice?

No. United Traffic Tickets Defense is a document-preparation and case-tracking service. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice. Documents are reviewed by a licensed professional before filing.

Does the free scan guarantee my ticket will be dismissed?

No. The scan is always free, but no document preparation or legal service can guarantee a court outcome. Results depend on the court, judge, officer participation, and the facts of your case.

What file types can I upload for the scan?

The scan accepts JPG, PNG, HEIC, and PDF documents. For best results, use a clear, well-lit photo with all fields on the citation visible.

Is my citation photo and driver's-license information private?

Yes. Ticket photos, driver's-license information, and dates of birth are treated as sensitive data, stored in access-controlled storage, and never sold. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What should I do if I have an upcoming court date or bench warrant?

Do not ignore a court date or active bench warrant. Contact the relevant court as soon as possible. See our Contact page for emergency guidance on warrants, court deadlines, DUI matters, and suspended licenses.
