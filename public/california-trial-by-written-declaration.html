California workflow • TBWD / online trial by declaration

Fight a California traffic ticket in writing.

When a California infraction is eligible, a Trial by Written Declaration can let you contest the citation without appearing in court. United Traffic Tickets can organize the citation, identify the applicable court workflow, prepare the document package for review, and keep the case timeline visible.

Important: TBWD is an option for eligible California traffic matters, not a requirement for every ticket. Court procedures can vary, and the official court instructions control. This page is an informational workflow guide, not legal advice and not a court filing.
1

Scan the citation

Upload the ticket. The scanner extracts the citation number, court or agency, violation, dates, and other visible fields so the case can be checked before documents are prepared.

2

Check the court workflow

The system should identify the jurisdiction and distinguish a standard written-declaration path from an online court program such as MyCitations where available.

3

Protect the deadline

California Rule of Court 4.210 establishes the written-declaration procedure and appearance-date rules. The case timeline should preserve the court's actual due date and any extension or court-specific instruction.

4

Prepare, review, file

California's mandatory-use forms include TR-205 (Request for Trial by Written Declaration), TR-215 (Decision and Notice of Decision), and related officer forms. Documents should be reviewed and explicitly approved before any filing step.

5

Track the court response

Once the filing is complete, the case can move through submitted, awaiting court, and decided stages. Status changes can trigger a customer email with a secure Case Center link.

6

Handle the result

If the declaration is unsuccessful, the next available procedure depends on how the matter was handled. For MyCitations online trial by declaration, the court's rules differ from the traditional paper workflow, so the decision notice must be followed.
