    Stop Sign Violations (VC 22450)

    Stop sign citations in California often hinge on visibility, proper signage, and what the officer could actually see. We help you review the citation and draft a written defense.

    When a stop sign ticket is worth fighting

    A speeding conviction in California adds a point to your driving record, can raise your insurance, and incurs court costs in addition to the fine. For commercial drivers, even one "reportable" conviction can threaten a CDL. Many citations are eligible for dismissal — but only where defects on the written citation are properly raised and the court agrees. We do not guarantee any dismissal.

    What your citation gets checked for

    • Signage & visibility — whether the sign is fully visible and unobstructed at the position where you should stop..
    • Officer line of sight — the officer must have a clear, continuous view to make the observation..
    • The stopping point — where the stop line or crosswalk actually is vs. where you stopped..
    • Code section accuracy — whether VC 22450(a) is correctly applied to the location..
    • Conditions at the time — poor light, weather, or construction can all undermine the citation..

    How written-declaration defense works

    We prepare a written declaration that frames the visibility and observation facts in a structured defense.

    We prepare documents and do not appear in court. We cannot guarantee a dismissal; every court decides on its own.
