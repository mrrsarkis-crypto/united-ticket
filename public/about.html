    Who We Are

    About United Traffic Tickets Defense

    A California document-preparation and case-tracking service founded in 2015 — not a law firm — helping drivers understand their options and prepare the paperwork to address their ticket.

    What we do

    United Traffic Tickets + Defense (California) helps drivers across Los Angeles County and every county and courthouse in California prepare and track the paperwork for their traffic matters. We focus on the practical side of the process: gathering the right information from your citation, explaining your options, preparing the documents you need, and tracking your case from scan to resolution.

    We are not a law firm. We are a document preparation and case-management service. We do not provide legal advice, and we never guarantee outcomes. Where legal representation or advice is needed, we work with qualified attorneys and guide you to the right resource.

    Founded in 2015

    The company was founded in 2015 to make the confusing, paperwork-heavy side of California traffic tickets more manageable for everyday drivers. Since then, we have focused on one thing: helping people turn a stressful citation into a clear, step-by-step plan with professional support along the way.

    Who runs the business

    United Traffic Tickets Defense is led by Sarkis Tulumdzhyan, Founder / Owner / CEO. Sarkis built the service around the belief that no driver should feel lost when holding a traffic citation — everyone deserves to understand their options in plain English.

    Who reviews your file

    When you use our service, your file is prepared with care and then reviewed by a California Certified Paralegal. For matters that require a courtroom appearance, attorneys licensed in California review the file and go to court for the client, as needed. This separation is important: document preparation and review are handled by trained, certified professionals, while licensed attorneys step in when your matter requires legal representation.

    Small Business Enterprise (SBE) certified

    United Traffic Tickets Defense is a certified Small Business Enterprise (SBE). Our SBE certification number is 2053854. This reflects our status as a qualified small business in California.

    Where we serve

    Our primary focus is all of Los Angeles County, covering the full range of courts — from Van Nuys and Chatsworth to Stanley Mosk in Downtown LA and beyond. We also work with drivers across all counties and courthouses in California.

    What our clients say

    We are proud of the real feedback our clients share. You can read genuine five-star reviews on our Yelp page — reviews from drivers we have actually helped. We never use invented testimonials.

    Not legal advice

    If you have an active warrant, a DUI or serious charge, an urgent court deadline, or a commercial driving license at stake, please contact a licensed attorney or the relevant court promptly. Nothing on this site is a substitute for legal advice.

    Our commitment

    Whether you are responding to a speeding ticket, sorting out a failure to appear, or preparing for a court date, our goal is the same: to be clear, honest, and practical about your options. Contact us any time for a free, no-pressure conversation.
