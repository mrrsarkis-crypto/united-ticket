    Cell Phone & Distracted Driving Tickets

    California’s hands-free law (VC 23123 and related) is aggressively enforced. We help you review the citation for stop-legality and evidence defects and prepare a written defense.

    Hands-free citations & what to check

    A speeding conviction in California adds a point to your driving record, can raise your insurance, and incurs court costs in addition to the fine. For commercial drivers, even one "reportable" conviction can threaten a CDL. Many citations are eligible for dismissal — but only where defects on the written citation are properly raised and the court agrees. We do not guarantee any dismissal.

    What we check on your citation

    • Legal basis for the stop — whether the officer had grounds to pull you over in the first place..
    • The cited section — VC 23123 (hand-held) vs. 23123.5 (teen), 23124, or "distracted driving" — the wrong section is a common error..
    • What the officer observed — a credible, unobstructed view of the specific violation is required..
    • Device-in-hand distinction — using a phone for navigation or mounted use is legal in some circumstances..
    • Citation completeness — the officer and device blocks must be complete and legible..

    How written-declaration defense works

    We prepare a written declaration that lays out the facts of how the device was used and the defects in the citation.

    We prepare documents and do not appear in court. Outcomes vary; we cannot guarantee dismissal.
