Why a CDL ticket is different

A speeding conviction in California adds a point to your driving record, can raise your insurance, and incurs court costs in addition to the fine. For commercial drivers, even one "reportable" conviction can threaten a CDL. Many citations are eligible for dismissal — but only where defects on the written citation are properly raised and the court agrees. We do not guarantee any dismissal.

What matters for a commercial citation

  • Reportability — whether the violation is FMCSA-reportable and how it affects your CDL..
  • 35-mph-over & serious thresholds — special thresholds that trigger serious-offense treatment..
  • The underlying proof — the officer, equipment, and citation completeness all matter..
  • Code section accuracy — an incorrect section can mean a conviction you shouldn’t have received..
  • Carrier & employer impact — how a conviction may appear on your driving record and to your employer..
