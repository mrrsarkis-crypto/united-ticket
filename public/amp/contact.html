Emergency guidance

If you are in a time-sensitive situation, the steps below may help. This guidance is general information, not legal advice.

Active bench warrant / failure to appear

  • Do not ignore it. An outstanding bench warrant can lead to further penalties, hold on your record, and possibly a red light on your driving record.
  • Contact the court that issued the warrant as soon as possible to learn the next steps and any lawful ways to address the FTA.
  • If you are unsure of the court, our courthouse directory can help you locate it.

Upcoming court date

  • Missing a scheduled court date can result in additional charges or a warrant. Act before the date; do not wait until the day of.
  • If you cannot appear, ask the court about legally available alternatives such as appearing remotely or requesting a continuance.

DUI charges

  • DUI matters are serious and often time-sensitive. Promptly seek a licensed attorney experienced in DUI defense.
  • If you were arrested, note the date and preserve any documentation you received.

Suspended or revoked license

  • Driving with a suspended or revoked license carries serious penalties. Do not drive unless you are authorized to.
  • Contact the DMV and the court to understand the terms of the suspension or revocation and any reinstatement requirements.
  • This service can help with preparation of reinstatement paperwork — see our suspended license page.

In an emergency, or if you believe your driving privileges are at immediate risk, always contact the relevant court or a licensed attorney first.
