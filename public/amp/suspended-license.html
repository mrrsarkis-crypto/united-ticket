Why a suspended license matters

A speeding conviction in California adds a point to your driving record, can raise your insurance, and incurs court costs in addition to the fine. For commercial drivers, even one "reportable" conviction can threaten a CDL. Many citations are eligible for dismissal — but only where defects on the written citation are properly raised and the court agrees. We do not guarantee any dismissal.

What reinstatement paperwork depends on

  • The reason for the hold — unpaid tickets, FTA, insurance (SR-22), DUI, or a DMV action — each needs a different response..
  • DMV vs. court — whether the suspension is a court hold or a DMV administrative action..
  • Reinstatement fee(s) — any fees or proof required before the license can be reissued..
  • The citation itself — a VC 14601 ticket often has its own defects worth reviewing..
  • Complete documentation — incomplete proof is the most common reason reinstatement is delayed..
