What is a fix-it ticket?

A speeding conviction in California adds a point to your driving record, can raise your insurance, and incurs court costs in addition to the fine. For commercial drivers, even one "reportable" conviction can threaten a CDL. Many citations are eligible for dismissal — but only where defects on the written citation are properly raised and the court agrees. We do not guarantee any dismissal.

What matters for a fix-it ticket

  • Proof of correction — providing a signed correction is usually required to avoid the full fine..
  • Was it truly correctable? — some charges are mistakenly marked correctable but shouldn’t be — or vice versa..
  • Expired registration nuance — registration-related citations have specific rules about timing and proof..
  • Court appearance costs — clearing it without a costly appearance is often possible..
  • The underlying citation — even minor tickets can carry procedural defects worth reviewing..
