    Accessibility

    Accessibility Statement

    We are committed to making our website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities.

    Our commitment

    United Traffic Tickets Defense strives to ensure that its website is accessible to people with a wide range of abilities, including those who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, and magnifiers.

    What we aim to provide

    • Semantic markup with proper headings, landmarks, and labeled form controls so assistive technology can navigate the site.
    • Keyboard accessibility, so you can navigate all content and interactive elements without a mouse.
    • Readable text and color contrast to support low-vision users.
    • Text alternatives (alt text) for meaningful images and icons.
    • Legible resizable text, so the site works with browser zoom.

    Ongoing efforts

    Accessibility is an ongoing process. We regularly review our pages and work to align with recognized best practices, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) where practical for a small business. We also build pages from an established template, which keeps structure consistent across the site.

    Let us know if you need help

    If you experience any difficulty using our site, or if you would like assistance with your case another way, please contact us. We are glad to help over the phone and by email.
